Quick safety checklist

  • Never pay to get hired. Upfront activation or refundable fees are a warning sign.
  • Keep payment on approved channels. Moving off-platform may remove protections.
  • Never share passwords or OTP codes. Legitimate clients do not need your account credentials.
  • Be careful with free test work. Agree on a small, clearly limited sample or paid test.
  • Verify independently. Look up the company using sources you find yourself.
